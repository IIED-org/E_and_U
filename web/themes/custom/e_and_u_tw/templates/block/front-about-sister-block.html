About E&U

Environment & Urbanization is prepared by IIED's Human Settlements Group and published by Sage.

IIED is an independent research organisation working with partners on global challenges including urban poverty, inequality, climate change and sustainable development. E&U reflects this work by connecting research and practical experience from Africa, Asia and Latin America.

See IIED for institutional context, related resources and updates.

Sister journal

Medio Ambiente y Urbanización is an open access Journal focusing on Latin America. It was founded in 1983 to encourage researchers, NGO staff and other professionals to write about their work and to exchange ideas.
